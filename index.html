About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the AI Lab at Chonnam National University, advised by Prof. Chang Gyoon Lim. My research develops machine learning methods for complex physical systems, combining spatio-temporal graph neural networks, mixture-of-experts architectures, federated learning, and multimodal data fusion.

Through four national R&D projects (NRF, TIPA, INNOPOLIS) I have worked across the full research cycle — from method design and publication to production-grade deployment — on problems including solar forecasting, virtual power plants, satellite–ground data integration, and anomaly detection for critical infrastructure. My current work extends to small language models and large multimodal models through an active industry–academia project, and my interests span generative AI, physical AI and robotics, AI for scientific discovery, digital twins, and trustworthy AI.
