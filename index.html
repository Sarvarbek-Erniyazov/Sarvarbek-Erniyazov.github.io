About

Researcher by training,
engineer by habit.

I am a Ph.D. candidate at the AI Lab, Chonnam National University (advisor: Prof. Chang Gyoon Lim). My core research applies deep learning — spatio-temporal graph neural networks, federated learning, mixture-of-experts — to energy forecasting and anomaly detection, inside Korean national R&D programs (TIPA, NRF, INNOPOLIS) on virtual power plants, satellite–ground data integration, and AI-based monitoring of power infrastructure.

What defines my work is convergence range: taking that same deep-learning toolkit into a new domain and making it produce results there — medical image segmentation, crowded-scene detection, battery quality control, industrial control security, document intelligence, audio & vision deepfake defense. Right now I am extending that range to LLM agents and multimodal AI (VPP-Copilot, Sci-RAG).

I started in hardware — an M.S. on QCA low-power circuit design at Kumoh (nine publications, one cited 88 times) — so I think from the physical system up: sensors, signals, constraints, deployment.

My rule: research only counts when it runs. Every project on this page is built end-to-end — leakage-audited data splits, honest ablations (including negative results), test suites and CI, and a live deployed demo wherever the work allows one. Seven demos are online right now.
